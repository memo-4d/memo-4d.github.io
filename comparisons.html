Main Page
  • Each video shows the Gaussian render on the left and the mesh extracted at every frame on the right, shaded above and as normals below.
  • Drag across a video to move the split. MEMO is on its left and the chosen baseline on its right, in every panel.
  • The baseline method buttons choose the method compared with MEMO.
  • Supported browsers: Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Safari (M3 chip Macs or later and iPhone 15 Pro or later)
